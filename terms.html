Program Name

YAP-IT AI Receptionist Notifications.

Program Description

YAP-IT provides an AI phone receptionist that answers calls on behalf of a business when the business cannot. When the assistant takes an enquiry from a caller, this program sends a text message to the business owner so they can return the call.

Each message contains the details the assistant collected: the caller's name, the caller's phone number, the service they asked about, and any notes they gave. The messages are transactional notifications about the recipient's own business. They contain no marketing, no promotions, and no links.

Who Receives Messages

Only the account holder. Messages are sent solely to the mobile number that a business owner has entered themselves in the account settings of their YAP-IT dashboard, and only about enquiries taken for that owner's own business.

No numbers are imported, purchased, or added from any other source, and no message is ever sent to a caller or to any third party.

How to Join

A business owner enters their own mobile number in their YAP-IT dashboard, in the field marked as the number where messages taken by the assistant are sent. Entering that number is what starts the notifications; it is the only way to join this program.

Message Frequency

One message per enquiry the assistant takes. Frequency therefore varies with how many calls a business receives — typically a few messages per day. There is no recurring or scheduled send.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's standard rates for receiving text messages apply to these notifications. YAP-IT does not charge you for the messages themselves; check with your carrier about the cost of messages on your plan.

How to Stop Messages

Reply STOP to any message from this program to stop receiving them. You will receive one confirmation message, and no further messages will be sent to that number.

You can also remove or change the number at any time in your YAP-IT dashboard, which stops notifications to it.

To start receiving messages again after replying STOP, reply START from the same handset.
