Program Name

YAP VOICE AI Receptionist Notifications.

Program Description

YAP VOICE provides an AI phone receptionist that answers calls on behalf of a business when the business cannot. When the assistant takes an enquiry from a caller, this program sends a text message to the business owner so they can return the call.

Each message contains the details the assistant collected: the caller's name, the caller's phone number, the service they asked about, and any notes they gave. The messages are transactional notifications about the recipient's own business. They contain no marketing, no promotions, and no links.

Every message begins with the sender name YAP-AI Solutions and ends with opt-out instructions.

Who Receives Messages

Business owners only: at the mobile number they entered themselves in the account settings of their YAP VOICE dashboard, and only about enquiries taken for their own business.

Callers, and other members of the public, are never sent a text message under this program. No numbers are imported, purchased, or added from any other source.

How to Join

A business owner types their own mobile number into their YAP VOICE dashboard, in the field marked as the number where messages taken by the assistant are sent, and ticks the consent checkbox beside it. The field is empty and the checkbox unticked by default. Doing both is what starts the notifications; it is the only way to join this program. The form and its consent wording are shown, without a login, on the SMS Opt-In page.

Message Frequency

One message per enquiry the assistant takes. Frequency therefore varies with how many calls a business receives — typically a few messages per day. There is no recurring or scheduled send.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's standard rates for receiving text messages apply to these notifications. YAP VOICE does not charge you for the messages themselves; check with your carrier about the cost of messages on your plan.

How to Stop Messages

Reply STOP to any message from this program to stop receiving them. You will receive one confirmation message, and no further messages will be sent to that number.

You can also remove or change the number at any time in your YAP VOICE dashboard, which stops notifications to it.

To start receiving messages again after replying STOP, reply START from the same handset.

Supported Carriers

Messages are sent within the United States and are supported on major US carriers. Carrier support may change without notice.

Privacy

Mobile numbers collected for this program are used only to send the messages described above. They are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and are never used for marketing. See the Privacy Policy for details of what is collected and how it is handled.
