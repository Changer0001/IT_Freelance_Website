Restaurant demo terms

Last updated: November 18, 2026

The restaurant demo lets you hear YAP Voice answer as a restaurant, using what that restaurant publishes on its own website.

Google Maps

The search box on the demo page uses Google Maps features and content. Your use of them is subject to the current Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Search results are shown to you and are not stored by YAP Voice.

What the demo reads

It reads public pages of the restaurant's own website: its name, address, hours and some of its menu. It does not read prices.

The call

The demo answers only the phone number you enter, for 15 minutes, and says it is a demonstration. Nothing said on the call is sent to the restaurant or anyone else.
