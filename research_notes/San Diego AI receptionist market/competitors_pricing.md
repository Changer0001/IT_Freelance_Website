# Competitive Landscape and Pricing: AI Phone Receptionists for Home-Service Trades and Restaurants (US, as of 2026-10-13)

**Method note (read first).** Direct fetching of vendor pages was blocked in this research session: WebFetch failed DNS lookup and curl got a 403 from the egress proxy for smith.ai and other sites. Reddit was also not searchable (WebSearch refuses reddit.com). So every figure below comes from web-search result summaries of the cited pages. Confidence labels used:
- **[V]** = the cited page is the vendor's own (help center, product page, blog), or a third-party page that explicitly says it read the vendor's pricing page on a stated date (for example "pricing page accessed Sept 2026"). Still confirm on the live page before quoting to a customer.
- **[3P]** = third-party estimate, review, or claim, or sources that conflict.
- Many "comparison" articles are written by competing AI-receptionist vendors (CloudTalk, Voksha, GetMacha, OnCrew, ServiceAgent, GetNextPhone, RainVoice, Dialzara, Loman, Kea, Hostie, Upfirst, Claudessa). Treat them as biased, even when the numbers agree with each other.

## Q1. What do the named products charge, and how? (master competitor table)

### Takeaway
Pricing falls into four groups. (1) Self-serve generic SMB AI at $25–$300/mo, billed per minute or per call (Upfirst, Dialzara, Rosie, My AI Front Desk, Goodcall, Smith.ai AI, Quo Sona, RingCentral). (2) Add-ons inside field-service software, $29/mo to per-call usage (Jobber $29 + $0.79/conversation; ServiceTitan $2.75/call; Housecall Pro quoted by sales). (3) Trade-specific "AI CSR" platforms at $449 to about $3,500/mo, sold through demos (Sameday, Avoca, Hatch, LeadTruffle). (4) Restaurant-specific flat per-location plans at $199–$599/mo (Hostie, Loman, Kea, Slang, Yelp Host). Human receptionist services cost about 5–10x more per call or minute (Ruby about $3.45–$5/min; Smith.ai human about $8.50–$11.50/call overage).

### Inferences
- The cheapest credible self-serve option for a one-truck trade is about $25–$60/mo (Upfirst Starter/Premium, Dialzara entry, Rosie Professional, Jobber add-on at $29 for existing Jobber users). That price buys message-taking and basic answering; booking and transfers usually need the $149+ tier (Rosie Scale, for example).
- Field-service software vendors (Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro) are absorbing AI answering as a cheap add-on. That squeezes standalone sellers whose pitch is "integrates with Jobber/HCP." A local reseller's edge would have to be setup, tuning, bilingual handling and local service, not the integration itself.
- Restaurant AI has settled around $199–$450 per location per month, about 2–4x a trade's entry AI plan, because ordering and POS integration carry more value.
- Per-call billing (Smith.ai, Upfirst, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Quo) favors trades, whose emergency calls often run 4–6 minutes. Per-minute bundles favor restaurants' short calls.

### Gaps
- Feature-level detail could not be confirmed from primary pages for most vendors: emergency flagging, text-to-owner, and native ServiceTitan/HCP/Jobber integrations for Goodcall, Rosie and Dialzara. Rosie and Dialzara seem to integrate mostly through calendars and Zapier, but this is unconfirmed.
- Housecall Pro CSR AI, Avoca, Hatch, Podium AI Employee, Loman, and Numa publish no prices; only estimates exist.
- Overage rates for Rosie, Sameday and Ruby are not published.
- No Google-branded AI receptionist for SMBs was found in the searches. The only consumer-side feature recalled from training is Google "Ask for Me," which calls businesses on a user's behalf; it is not confirmed here and is not a receptionist product.
- Reddit (r/HVAC, r/Plumbing, r/smallbusiness) could not be searched from this environment.

## Q3. Typical monthly cost for a small trade: AI receptionist vs a traditional human answering service

### Takeaway
A small trade typically pays about **$30–$300/mo for AI** (most land around $50–$200 for 50–300 calls). Human answering costs about **$200–$450/mo for only 50–100 included minutes**, with overage of **$1.25–$3.30/min** (Ruby and Smith.ai human run higher: about $3.45–$5.40/min or $8.50–$11.50/call). Per call, AI costs about $0.50–$3 and a human about $5–$12.

### Cited Findings
- Contractor guide: live services cost $200–$450/mo for 50–100 minutes, with $1.50–$2.50/min overage — [OnCallClerk](https://www.oncallclerk.com/blog/answering-service-cost-for-contractors) [3P]
- Industry guide: about $1–$2/min is typical after included minutes; live entry plans $200–$400/mo — [OnCrew](https://oncrew.ai/resources/contractor-answering-service-cost) [3P]
- AnswerConnect: $350/mo for 200 min, $2.50/min overage, $49.99 setup; $395 for 300 min with $1.85 overage; rounds up to the full minute (read from the vendor price list, Aug–Sept 2026) — [Voksha](https://voksha.com/pricing-comparison/answerconnect/), [FitMyCall](https://fitmycall.com/answerconnect/pricing/). A conflicting 3P figure is $189/50 min.
- PATLive: conflicting — $49/mo + $2.99/min with a 14-day trial; or about $199/75 min with about $1.85 overage; or $1.49–$1.79/min — [Upfirst blog](https://upfirst.ai/blog/how-much-does-an-answering-service-cost), [GetNextPhone](https://www.getnextphone.com/blog/answering-service-companies) [3P]
- Abby Connect: $165/50 min with $3.30 overage, or $329/100 min with $2.99 overage; AI Starter $99/50 min — [GetAira](https://www.getaira.io/blog/best-answering-service-small-business) [3P, conflicting]
- AnswerFirst (home services): $1.60–$1.95/min + $30/mo, billed per second — [AnswerFirst](https://answerfirst.com/service-providers/) [V]
- Ruby: $250/50 min ≈ $5/min at entry, falling to $3.45/min on the 500-min plan — [Voksha](https://voksha.com/pricing-comparison/ruby/)
- Emergency plumbing calls run 4–6 minutes, so a single call on a $0.48–$1.96/min plan costs $1.92–$11.76 (vendor-authored analysis) — [Claudessa](https://claudessa.com/blog/how-much-does-an-answering-service-cost)
- Retail SMB AI plans: $49–$325/mo — [CentriCall](https://www.centricallai.com/article-and-insight/white-label-ai-voice-agents)

### Inferences
- Example: 100 calls/mo at about 3 minutes each (300 min). AI: Upfirst Pro $159.95, Rosie Scale $149, Smith.ai AI about $210–$270, ServiceTitan $275, Jobber $29 + 70×$0.79 ≈ $84. Human: Ruby 300 min falls between the $720 and $1,725 tiers, so about $1,000+ in practice; AnswerConnect $395; Smith.ai human about $300 + 70×~$9 ≈ $900+. AI comes out roughly 3–6x cheaper.
- A San Diego reseller pricing at $199–$299/mo flat sits above self-serve AI but well under human services. That is the band local competitors (PickupBell, Local Call AI) already use.

### Gaps
- Independent (non-vendor) survey data on what trades actually pay was not found. Reddit owner sentiment could not be gathered.
