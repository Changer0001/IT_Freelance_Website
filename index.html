How it works

What happens when someone calls

  1. 1

    Your phone rings first

    Calls reach you as they do today. If you don't pick up in the time you choose — or you're closed — the assistant answers. Or let it take every call.

  2. 2

    It answers like your front desk

    It says it's your business's automated assistant, then answers from what you've told it: hours, services, the area you cover.

  3. 3

    It takes the details

    Name, the best number to call back, what they need, and your own questions for that job — like which day suits them.

  4. 4

    You get a text and an email

    When the call ends, the enquiry is sent to you. It also waits in your dashboard with a button to call them back.

What it does

A receptionist that never puts anyone on hold

Every item here is something it does on calls today. You choose which of them your line uses.

Answers questions

Hours, services, the area you cover — from the information you give it. When it doesn't know, it says so and takes a message instead of guessing.

Takes enquiries your way

Set the questions for each service — "what size is the TV?", "which day suits you?" — and every enquiry arrives with the answers.

Reads the number back

When a caller gives a callback number, it's read back to them to confirm, so you don't ring a stranger over one misheard digit.

Puts callers through

If someone asks for a person, it can pass the call to your phone — only if you've switched that on and given a number.

Takes bookings, if you want

Restaurants can let it take table bookings — name, party size, date and time — for you to confirm in the dashboard.

Honest with callers

It tells every caller it's an automated assistant, and tells anyone describing an emergency to hang up and call 911.

Questions

What owners ask first

Will callers know it's not a person?

Yes. Every call starts with it saying it's your business's automated assistant. If someone asks whether they're talking to a robot, it answers plainly.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep the number your customers know and forward the calls you want it to answer — every call, after hours only, or when you don't pick up.

What if it can't answer a question?

It says it doesn't know rather than making something up, takes the caller's details, and you get them by text and email like any other enquiry.

Are calls recorded?

No. There's no audio recording and no transcript is kept. You get the details the caller gave — name, number, what they need — and a short note of what they asked for. The privacy policy has the full detail.

How is it set up, and what does it cost?

We set it up with you: your services, hours, the questions you want asked, and where enquiries should go. Pricing depends on your call volume — book a demo and you'll get a quote before anything is switched on.
