Plans, Billing and Cancellation

Last updated: November 23, 2026

YAP VOICE is a product of YAP-AI Solutions, San Diego, CA. YAP VOICE and YAP-AI Solutions are trade names of Burak Yilmaz, San Diego, CA ("I", "me", or "my"). This page says what a business pays for YAP VOICE, when it pays, how to cancel, and what happens when it does. It is written to be read in a few minutes.

Plans and Prices

  • Answering: $99 a month for each business phone line. The assistant answers the calls you send it, takes the caller's name, number and what they need, answers questions from the information you give it, and texts and emails you after each call.
  • Answering + Booking: $149 a month for each business phone line. Everything in Answering, and the assistant offers callers your open times and books, moves and cancels visits in your own Google Calendar.
  • A restaurant's line is on the $99 plan. Taking table requests is part of answering a restaurant's calls.

There is no setup fee and no minimum term. Prices are in US dollars. If the law requires me to collect a tax on the service, it is shown before you pay.

How Many Calls a Plan Covers

Each plan covers up to 300 calls a month on its line. If a month goes over, the assistant keeps answering and you are not charged extra for that month.

If your line takes more than 300 calls a month for two months in a row, I will write to you and we agree a price that fits before anything changes. If we cannot agree, either of us can end the service at the end of the month you have paid for.

The 14-Day Free Trial

  • The trial is on either plan and starts the day your line is switched on.
  • You pay nothing during the trial, and you do not need to give a card to start it.
  • Before the trial ends I send you a payment link. If you want to carry on, you pay through it and your first paid month starts the day after the trial ends.
  • If you do not want to carry on, tell me or simply do not pay. The line is switched off when the trial ends and you owe nothing.

Paying

  • You pay monthly, in advance, by card, through Stripe. I never see or store your card number.
  • It renews automatically. Your card is charged the plan's price on the same day each month until you cancel.
  • Stripe emails you a receipt for every payment.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe tries the card again and I write to you. If it is still unpaid after 7 days, the line may be paused until it is paid. A paused line is not answered by the assistant.
  • If a price is going to change, I tell you by email at least 30 days before, and you can cancel before the new price starts.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel at any time, for any reason, with no fee. Email yap.itsupport@gmail.com from the address on your account, or tell me by phone or text.
  • Cancelling stops the next charge. The assistant keeps answering until the end of the month you have already paid for, unless you ask me to switch it off sooner.
  • If your own phone number forwards calls to the assistant, turn that forwarding off when you leave. I will show you how.

Refunds

  • A month that has already started is not refunded when you cancel.
  • If you were charged by mistake, or charged after you had cancelled, I refund that charge in full.

Your Information When You Leave

When your account closes, your phone line is released and the information held for your business is deleted, as the privacy policy describes. If you would like a copy of your setup and your enquiries first, ask before you cancel.

Changes to This Page

If these terms change, the date at the top changes, and customers are told by email at least 30 days before a change that affects what they pay or how they cancel.

Contact

Questions about a charge or about cancelling: yap.itsupport@gmail.com.
