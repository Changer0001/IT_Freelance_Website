Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

YAP VOICE ("I", "me", or "my") operates this website to provide information about IT support services and to allow potential clients to get in touch. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information I Collect

The only personal information I collect is what you voluntarily submit through the contact form:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • The message you send describing your IT issue or project

I do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or any third-party tracking scripts on this website.

How I Use Your Information

The information you submit is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Provide you with a quote or schedule a service visit

I will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing purposes.

Form Submission Service

Contact form submissions are processed through Web3Forms, a third-party service that forwards form submissions to my email. Please review their Privacy Policy for details on how they handle form data.

Phone Calls Answered by the AI Assistant

When you call a business that uses YAP VOICE, the call may be answered by an automated assistant rather than a person. The assistant says so at the start of the call.

The call is not recorded. No audio of the conversation is captured, stored, or kept anywhere.

No transcript of the call is kept. The back-and-forth of the conversation is held only while the call is happening, and is gone when you hang up. Nobody can read back what was said.

What the business does keep is the outcome of the call and the details you gave for it to act on — typically your name, your phone number, the service you asked about, and the answers to any questions about the job. Alongside those, a short note of what you said you needed is kept, in case the details alone do not capture it — for example if you asked about something the business does not list. That note is a summary, not a transcript of the call. All of it is the information the business needs in order to call you back, and it is covered in the section below.

To understand what you say, the assistant sends the audio of the call to a speech-recognition service and the text of what you said to a language model, both operated by third parties, for the duration of the call. These services are used to process the call as it happens.

If you would prefer to speak to a person, say so and the assistant will put you through or take a message for someone to call you back.

SMS Notifications for AI Receptionist Clients

Businesses that use the YAP VOICE AI phone receptionist can choose to be notified by text message when the assistant takes an enquiry for them. This section covers that separately, because it involves information the website contact form does not.

For that service I collect and store:

  • The business owner's own mobile number, which they enter themselves in their YAP VOICE dashboard in order to receive these notifications
  • The details each caller gives the assistant — their name, their phone number, the service they asked about, and any notes

The owner's mobile number is used for one purpose only: sending that owner notifications about enquiries taken for their own business. It is never used for marketing, and it is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party. An owner can remove or change it in the dashboard at any time, or reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them.

Caller details are stored so the business can return the call, and are shown only to that business. A caller's information is never shared with any other business on the platform, and is never used for marketing.

Text messages are delivered through Twilio, which carries the message to the recipient's mobile carrier. See Twilio's Privacy Notice for how they handle message data. Full SMS Terms and Conditions are published separately, and the SMS Opt-In page shows exactly how and where a business owner consents.

Where the Service Runs

The YAP VOICE phone assistant and the business dashboard run on equipment I operate. Connections to it — a business owner opening their dashboard, and the phone network passing a call to the assistant — travel through Cloudflare, which provides the encrypted connection between the internet and that equipment. This means the audio of a call answered by the assistant, and the pages of the dashboard, pass through Cloudflare's network as they happen. The enquiry records themselves are stored only in the YAP VOICE system, not by Cloudflare. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for how it handles data passing through its network.

Dashboard accounts are created by me for each business. Each is protected by a password the account holder can change at any time from their dashboard, and a business owner's account shows only their own business's calls and enquiries.

Data Retention

Your contact information is retained only as long as needed to address your inquiry. It is stored in my email inbox and is not copied to any additional database or CRM.

For the AI receptionist service, enquiry records and the owner's notification number are retained while the business holds an active account, and are deleted on request or when the account is closed.

Calls are not recorded and conversations are not stored, so there is nothing of either to retain. A record that a call happened — when it was, how long it lasted, and how it ended — is kept for up to 24 months so the business has its own history; it contains none of what was said.
